# Reader Survey

A chapter-specific survey for readers of *An Afterlife for Atheists*.

## Language

**Chapter**:
A numbered section of the book with its own title and reader responses. Its identity stays the same if its title changes.

**Chapter rating**:
A reader's score from 0 to 10 for how convincing they found a chapter's case for the existence of an afterlife.
_Avoid_: Belief score, writing-quality score, book review

**Reader**:
A person visiting the survey to view or contribute responses to a chapter.
_Avoid_: Customer, subscriber

**Comment**:
An optional public statement about one chapter, independent of a chapter rating.
_Avoid_: Reply, private feedback

**Chapter results**:
The response count, average score, and distribution of the current ratings for one chapter.
_Avoid_: Proof, consensus, unique-person count

**Report**:
A reader's request for an administrator to review a comment for possible abuse.

**Administrator**:
The person authorized to manage chapter information, review results, export responses, and moderate comments.

**Display name**:
An optional, unverified name or nickname shown beside a comment. A comment without one is attributed to “Anonymous reader.”
