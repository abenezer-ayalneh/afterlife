# Homepage compositions

The user pinned the visual world: quiet literary; warm off-white/charcoal; book-like serif headings; clean sans controls; muted earthy accent; original abstract ink artwork; no slogans. The homepage's mode is Operate: choose the relevant chapter and enter its survey. It is not a marketing page.

Seven viable structures, ordered by resonance, within that pinned world:

1. Contents page: compact book-title/ink introduction above a numbered nine-row chapter index.
2. Two-page spread: introduction and ink study in the left column; the full chapter index in the right column.
3. Margin study: a narrow chapter rail beside the title, explanation, and contained artwork; one-column mobile ordering.
4. Centered frontispiece: centered title, shallow ink artwork, and a three-column chapter index underneath.
5. Horizontal register: compact title and illustration across the top, then chapter rows divided into three numbered groups.
6. Chapter directory: left-aligned compact introduction above a two-column row-based chapter list and narrow artwork margin.
7. Reading-room index: shallow title and copy at the top, compact full-width illustration, then an uninterrupted vertical chapter index.

Each includes only the book title, factual introduction, direct chapter links, a theme control, and policy footer. No invented scores or chapter names, promotional sections, quotes, pricing, book purchase flow, or newsletter.
